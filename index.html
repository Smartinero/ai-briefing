🧭 Themes — who covered what

1 · Faster and more hands-free

@bot's Oct 17 thread claims computer tasks finish about three times sooner. Voice got the most attention. xAI's Grok Voice 3.0 article (Oct 14) says the new model "also powers Grok Bot's voice mode". @bot says calls now remember past conversations. On an iPhone 15 Pro or later with app version 1.16, you can set the Action Button to start a voice chat (Oct 20 post). The changelog adds that a dropped voice call now reconnects and carries on. A phone call also wakes a sleeping Bot computer, so the Bot can act on what you said after you hang up.

Notifications changed too. Per the updated "Settings and notifications" page (Oct 17), while you use the desktop app on a Mac or Windows PC, alerts go there and not to your phone. If a Bot needs your input, your phone is notified about 3 minutes later unless you open that Bot first. Assessment: if you want phone alerts while at your desk, that delay is by design, not a fault.

2 · Your Bot's own inbox and accounts grow up

Two releases fill in the email inbox announced last edition. In 0.70.2 (Oct 11), email the Bot sends from its own address waits for you on a draft card. When a sign-in emails a code or link, the Bot reads it from your connected Gmail or Outlook, or from its own inbox, instead of asking you. If more than one address could send, it asks which one, then tells you which it used. In 0.71.2 (Oct 14), plus-address tags let one inbox serve several purposes, each with its own routine. Brock gave the inbox a chapter in his Oct 16 news episode.

Elsewhere, Bots can pay with X Money or Stripe Link, and at checkout they use card details or photos you send in chat. They can read X Chat once X is connected. Apps like Figma, Notion and Canva can show up as an interactive card in the chat. If you paste a connector link, the Bot installs the matching plugin itself. Assessment, not a source claim: payments and inbound mail are where mistakes cost money. Keep approval on for anything that sends or pays.

3 · Learning from how SpaceXSI runs its own Bots

There is a lot of worked-example material this time. Brock walked through six of the official staff guides. xAI News published "Using Grok Bot to detect and respond to incidents" (Oct 20), in which two support Bots share the job. IncidentWatch checks the last 15 minutes of tickets every five minutes and alerts a person. IncidentSweep prepares a batch of replies, shows the exact message and ticket list for approval, then sends in small batches, stopping on any failure. The article's own summary: "Give Bots jobs, not tasks." Nate Herk named Grok Bot one of the seven tools he relies on. His examples: posting a call transcript to his community after each Q&A, announcing each new video, and an AI-news pinger.

The updated "Skills and routines" page (Oct 12) has a checklist called "Design routines for trust". It says to have the Bot draft or recommend first, require approval for sending, buying or deleting, decide in advance what happens when data is missing or stale, and re-test after a website changes. Assessment: the same pattern runs through every example. One Bot owns one job, there is a checkpoint before anything goes out, and one front-door Bot reports to you.

4 · One plan for Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X

The X Subscription launched Oct 12. Paid plans share one usage pool across Grok, Grok Bot and Cursor, and the docs page "Grok Bot on The X Subscription" says Grok Bot usage then comes from that "weekly shared pool". What to try next (assessment)

TryStart one staff Bot instead of writing your own. Open the Grok Bot for Work guide, copy the Inbox Manager prompt into a new Bot, and leave it read-only. It is created switched off. Run Test run once and check that it picked current mail, then press Enable. Add Intel Scout a week later. Only add the "Bot Boss" front door once you have three Bots pinging you.

TryGive your Bot's inbox a second purpose with a tag. Ask: "Use the plus address +newsletters for any sign-ups, and create a routine that runs only on mail to that tag: every Monday, summarise what arrived in this chat and unsubscribe nothing without asking me." This needs app 0.71.2 or later.

TryTurn a chat report into a Page. Once your app has the update, ask: "Make this weekly summary a page, and have the routine update the page every Monday instead of posting a message." Shared pages download as PDF.

TryPin your schedule time zone. The Oct 11 notes say messaging from another time zone changes the zone your routines use, "unless you chose one under Timezone in Settings". Set it to Bangkok so trips don't move your routines.

DecideThe X Subscription. Check your billing settings for the switch offer. If you switch, pick Premium or higher, because Plus has no Grok Bot. Combining accounts "can't be undone, and Support can't undo it for you." Before you decide, note your current weekly usage under Settings → Usage & Billing.

📘 Grok Bot docs (official how-to) docs.x.ai/grok-bot
Skills and routines
DocsHigh relevance updated Oct 12 · ~6 min read
The practical parts this time. Event triggers (a Slack message, a GitHub notification) should use a narrow rule. The page warns against "every new message", because broad listeners "consume usage". Test run does real work, so test with safe inputs. To manage routines, open the Bot → View conversation details → Routines (pause, test, edit, see history). Limits: 50 routines per Bot, the last 20 run records kept, schedules at least five minutes apart, and deleting has no undo. After a long time away, Grok Bot may ask whether to keep routines running and pause them if you don't answer.
Message and collaborate
DocsHigh relevance updated Oct 17 · ~7 min read
Group chats take 2–6 Bots. A sample kickoff: "@Researcher gather the source material and link every claim. @Writer turn the findings into a launch draft. @Reviewer check the draft … Do not publish anything." Catches: approval cards, logins and email drafts do not appear in groups, and handoffs between Bots are text-only. A direct message from you redirects work in progress, and "Stop now" ends it, though it doesn't undo what's done. Tips listed: lead with the result you need, link the source of truth, ask for evidence. Shortcuts: Cmd/Ctrl+K search, Cmd/Ctrl+L adds selected text to the chat, Cmd/Ctrl+D dictates.
Settings and notifications
Docs updated Oct 17 · ~6 min read
The new notification rules. While the desktop app is in use (open, unlocked, used in the last 5 minutes), alerts from your Bots go to the desktop. Finished replies skip your phone. Questions reach the phone about 3 minutes later unless you open that Bot first. Switch alerts per Bot under View conversation details → Bot settings → Notifications. The page also says "Cursor manages model selection, so there is no model picker."
