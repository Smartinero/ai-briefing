Blog ⭐ High Relevance Jun 9 · ~8 min read
The headline release of the week. Key points for your workflow:

Fable 5 (generally available): A Mythos-class model — the tier above Opus — made safe for general use. State-of-the-art on nearly all capability benchmarks, with the biggest lead on long, complex, long-horizon tasks. Stronger at vision, memory and autonomous multi-step work. Priced at $10 / $50 per million input/output tokens.

Rollout (read this): Free on Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans through June 22. On June 23 it leaves those plans and requires usage credits until capacity allows it back as standard. If you want to stress-test Fable 5 on a book or PPC project, do it before the cutover.

Safeguards: New classifiers route requests touching cybersecurity, biology/chemistry, or model distillation to Opus 4.8 instead (you're told when it happens). Anthropic says <5% of sessions trigger any fallback — but the safeguards are deliberately conservative, so benign requests sometimes get caught.

Memory: Giving Fable persistent file-based memory improved its performance ~3× more than it did for Opus 4.8 — a concrete data point for your handover-document / persistent-context approach.

Mythos 5: Same underlying model with cyber safeguards lifted; restricted to Project Glasswing cyber-defence partners (and soon select biology researchers). Not something you'll access, but it's why "Mythos" keeps appearing in the coverage below.
