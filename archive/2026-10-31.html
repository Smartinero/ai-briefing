⭐ Top Highlights

1
Vault: your Bots can now sign in and check out without seeing your secrets
🛠️ Changelog 0.75.5 · Oct 23 · also @bot (Oct 24), a new docs page (Oct 28) and Matt Palmer
Open Settings → Vault → New item to save a Login, Card or Contact info. The docs say "A Bot never sees a saved password, card number, or security code." Use the Hints field ("Use for work purchases") so a Bot picks the right entry. On desktop you can import a password-manager CSV export. The catch: Vault covers your whole account, so every Bot you own can use every item. Assessment: read the payment caveat in the docs card before you save a card. The docs and the Oct 26 changelog disagree on whether each purchase needs your OK.
2
Fewer approval cards, and your rules now live in Settings → Permissions
🛠️ Changelog 0.75.5, 0.75.8, 0.77.0 · Oct 23–26 · docs updated Oct 29
Auto-review now "asks less often for an action you asked for in your own words." Email and Slack messages you ask for in chat go out once Auto-review allows them, but sends from routines still wait as drafts. If you press Deny, the Bot stops until your next message. A Bot can offer to add or change one of your rules on a card you confirm. Settings → Permissions lists your rules under Always Ask and Allow without Asking. Approval cards now stay open 30 minutes, up from 10. The docs add that cards from a chat message don't expire at all. Assessment: fewer cards means each "Always Ask" rule you set matters more. Check that list once.
3
"Grok Bot can now use Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, and more apps on your phone."
𝕏 @bot · Oct 23 · 2-post thread · details in the Mobile docs (Oct 28)
On iPhone and Android: Calendar (read, add, change, delete), Contacts (read-only), Location and Alarms. Reminders and Apple Home are iPhone only. It works only in your own chat with your own Bot, and only while the Grok Bot app is open on the phone. Allow apps ahead of time under Settings → Connect apps → iPhone (or Android). Limits: the Bot can't change repeating events, never invites anyone, and can't set an alarm more than 24 hours ahead. Assessment: good for quick tasks you'd dictate on the move. It won't work from a routine while the app is closed.
4
Grok Bot 0.78.1: Shopping cards and subscription templates
🛠️ Grok Bot changelog · Oct 27 · latest release
Shopping now shows product cards with photo, rating, price and store. "Buy now" starts an order you approve, and the Bot then tracks shipping. There are three new templates: "Try it, don't pay for it" (starts a free trial, then cancels or reminds you before it bills), "Find unused subscriptions" and "Watch subscription prices". The changelog says Bots "confirm with you before changing anything." Also in this release: routines made in a new Bot's first chat now use your time zone, not UTC.
5
Your next sales teammate should be a Grok Bot
📝 xAI News · Oct 27 · ~6 min read · also @bot
The company's sales team describes three Shared Bots: Account Intel, Quote and Deal. The useful part is the rules. Bots "act with each user's permissions, with additional restrictions on sensitive actions." Quote Bot "shows the rep a preview and asks for confirmation" before changing records. People review anything customer-facing, and any decision whose criteria can't be clearly defined. Assessment: the same pattern works for one person: preview first, confirm, then act. Copy it into your own routines.
🧭 Themes — who covered what

1 · Logins, cards and buying: the Bot as your errand runner

Four releases in five days build this out. Vault arrived in 0.75.5 (Oct 23). The 1Password connection now works without a shared vault, and you approve each login (0.77.0). The Vault gained a Payments tab, where you pick the payment method Bots use first, and address autocomplete (0.77.0). Product cards, order tracking and the subscription templates followed in 0.78.1 (Oct 27). The docs set the sign-in order: a Vault login first, then a connected password manager, then a Secure Form in chat. When you leave a Secure Form's "Securely save to Bot's vault" box unchecked, the value is used only in that chat. If you let a Bot pick the password when it signs up for a site, Vault generates and saves it, and the Bot never sees it. Matt Palmer's Oct 27 roundup leads with Vault and 1Password too.

Assessment: the sources disagree on one point, covered in the docs card below. The Oct 26 changelog says "you still approve every purchase." The Vault page (updated Oct 28) says a saved card "fills in at checkout without asking you each time, and no setting requires your approval for each use." Until that is settled, the safe reading is the docs' own advice. If you want to approve every payment, don't save the card.

Covered by 🛠️ Changelog (4 releases) 📘 Docs (new Vault page) 𝕏 @bot (Vault, 1Password) 🔍 Matt Palmer (weekly roundup)

2 · Fewer interruptions, with the rules in one place

Three releases cut approval cards. 0.75.5: Auto-review asks less for things you asked for in your own words, a denied card stops the Bot until your next message, and Auto-review "no longer waves through an action your request said to stop before, such as sending a message you asked only to draft." 0.75.8: Auto-review "takes a second look," saving or editing a routine no longer counts as risky by itself, and a Bot can propose a rule change on a card. 0.77.0: Settings → Permissions and 30-minute cards. The approvals page (Oct 29) explains what the cards mean. Allow once saves no rule. Always allow saves one to Custom Rules. Text from an email or web page "doesn't count as your request". Sending a new message closes any open card in that chat.

Assessment: your own wording now carries weight. "Draft only, don't send" is respected, and so is "send it". Say which you mean.

Covered by 🛠️ Changelog (0.75.5, 0.75.8, 0.77.0) 📘 Docs (approvals page) 🔍 Matt Palmer ("fewer approval cards")

3 · The Bot reaches into your phone

@bot announced phone apps on Oct 23. The Mobile docs (Oct 28) list what each app can do, and which phone the Bot uses if Grok Bot is open on two. It uses the phone you messaged from when it can. A "Grok Bot needs permission" card waits up to five minutes for you. If you allow the app within an hour, the Bot picks the request back up without being asked again. Brock gave it a chapter of his Oct 24 news episode, alongside Pages. Matt Palmer covers Apple Home, Reminders, Location, Contacts, Alarms and Calendar in his roundup. From the same docs page: on the phone you can now start a routine with Add routine on a Bot's profile. Testing a routine still needs the desktop app.

4 · Bots with one job, shared with a team

xAI News and @bot describe the sales team's three Shared Bots. The docs now have a Shared Bots page (new, Oct 28), though the changelog still says "Team Bot". Matt Palmer's Oct 23 video walks through two of his Team Bots, a marketing reviewer and a Slack-to-scheduled-post social Bot. Wes Roth's sponsored Oct 31 video builds a "chief of staff" Bot with specialist research Bots, group chats, routines and skills taught by demonstration. Docs details: in a Shared Bot, your chat is private from the owner, and the Bot asks before it uses your personal accounts (Allow once, Always allow, Skip). Each reply uses the usage of the person it answers.

Assessment: The docs say Shared Bots are "for members of a Cursor team who have Grok Bot", but the design pattern applies to personal Bots too. One Bot per job, a written rule for when a person must review, and a preview before any change.

What to try next (assessment)

TryReview your Permissions in two minutes. Update to 0.77.0 or later, open Settings → Permissions, and read both lists. Then ask your main Bot: "Add an Auto-review rule that always asks me before any purchase, payment or subscription change." It should answer with a rule card for you to confirm. Nothing changes until you do.

TryPut one login in the Vault, with a hint. Open Settings → Vault → New item → Login, save a site you use often, and under Hints write when to use it. Then ask: "Sign in to that site and tell me my next renewal date. Don't change anything." Cards: save one only if you're fine with it filling in at checkout without asking each time (see the docs card). Otherwise leave "Securely save to Bot's vault" unchecked when the Bot asks.

TryRun a subscription audit, read-only. Start the "Find unused subscriptions" template (0.78.1) and say: "List what I pay for and when each renews. Don't cancel anything; I'll pick." The changelog says Bots confirm before changing anything. The template can cancel plans you pick, so pick nothing on the first run.

TryLet the Bot use your phone. With the app open, go to Settings → Connect apps → iPhone (or Android) and allow Calendar. Then say: "Add 'Call the bank' to my calendar tomorrow at 10:00." Reminders need an iPhone. Contacts are read-only.

Approvals, security, and privacy
DocsHigh relevance updated Oct 29 · ~10 min read
A rewritten section on approval cards. "Most actions run on their own," and an action that matches one of your Ask first rules always gets a card. Warnings to watch for: "This can't be undone", "This changes sign-in or security settings" and "This shares your data outside this chat". Email from the Bot's own address: if you ask for it in chat, it goes out once Auto-review allows it. If a routine or an incoming message triggers it, it waits as a draft until you press Send. A forward, or an attachment Auto-review can't fully read, always waits for you. Cards from a chat message don't expire. Cards from background work expire after 30 minutes.
